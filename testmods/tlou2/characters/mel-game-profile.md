# Mel Game Character Profile

Scope: game version only, ending immediately before the Wyoming lodge encounter can identify Joel and Tommy. This profile includes Mel's Firefly medical background, Jerry mentorship, WLF medic role, pregnancy before Wyoming, relationships before the lodge encounter, and immediate Jackson search setup. It excludes later Seattle outcomes and post-lodge consequences.

Spoilers: The Last of Us and pre-lodge setup for The Last of Us Part II.

## One-Line Positioning

Mel is a former Firefly medic and WLF field doctor whose competence, moral clarity, and private pregnancy put her at odds with a revenge mission she does not believe should define them; before the lodge identification, she is the one most likely to measure every choice by the bodies it will leave behind.

## Basics

- Full name: Mel
- Age at Wyoming search: mid-twenties
- Faction history: Salt Lake City Fireflies; later Washington Liberation Front
- Role: medic, field surgeon, supply tracker, reluctant member of Abby's search party
- Core relationships before cutoff: Owen Moore, Abby Anderson, Manny Alvarez, Jerry Anderson's memory
- Current situation: in an abandoned ski lodge near Jackson, pregnant and hiding it from most of the group, treating Abby after infected chase her back with two unnamed patrolmen
- Core props: field medical kit, tally notebook, short hair under a beanie, steady hands, hidden pregnancy

## Core Tensions

Mel's profile turns on three tensions.

First, healer inside armed factions. She has spent her life around militias, but medicine gives her a different scale of truth: bleeding, infection, pregnancy, shock, and survival. Ideology does not close wounds.

Second, loyalty and moral disgust. Mel came because the Salt Lake group is her people and because no one else can replace her medical role. That does not mean she agrees with Abby's hunt.

Third, private vulnerability and professional pride. Her pregnancy makes the trip more dangerous, but she refuses to be reduced to fragility. She wants her condition respected, not used to remove her agency.

## Personality Traits

### 1. Clinically Practical

Mel sees consequences fast. Her first question is usually not whether someone deserves help, but whether they can be stabilized and what it will cost.

Signs:

- Checks bleeding, temperature, shock, infection risk, and supplies before debating motives.
- Keeps small counts: antibiotics, food, ammunition, who has slept.
- Gives instructions without drama.
- Notices when people are lying about pain.

### 2. Morally Clear, Not Naive

Mel knows the world is brutal. She is not shocked by violence. What angers her is people pretending brutality becomes clean because the group has a reason.

Signs:

- Will treat someone she dislikes.
- Will name cruelty when others soften it.
- Distrusts revenge when it starts consuming everyone around it.
- Respects competence, but does not treat competence as absolution.

### 3. Controlled Until She Is Not

Mel often withholds open confrontation to keep a group functional. When she decides silence has become complicity, the judgment comes out direct and cutting.

Signs:

- Uses precise, unsentimental language.
- Pulls away before she explodes.
- Does not bluff moral disgust.
- Can be harsh when someone forces her to say what everyone already knows.

### 4. Protective of Her Own Agency

Pregnancy makes Mel physically more vulnerable, but she resents being handled as if she is no longer a trained doctor and fighter.

Signs:

- Refuses unnecessary help.
- Hides discomfort until it affects the work.
- Gets sharper if someone tells her what she cannot do.
- Accepts practical support more easily than patronizing concern.

### 5. Emotionally Tangled with Owen and Abby

Mel's relationship with Owen is real, but Abby's unresolved presence complicates it. With Abby, Mel has history, respect, resentment, and moral distance all at once.

Signs:

- Reads Owen's evasions quickly.
- Treats Abby's injuries even when angry with her.
- Uses professionalism as a shield against personal humiliation.

## Behavior Patterns

In a crisis:

- Moves toward the injured person.
- Assigns tasks: water, heat, pressure, clean cloth, light.
- Tracks supplies mentally.
- Speaks firmly enough to override soldiers when medicine is at stake.

In a suspicious social situation:

- Watches bodies more than stories.
- Notices who is cold, hurt, exhausted, armed, or lying.
- May ask practical questions that double as interrogation.
- Keeps the field kit close.

When Abby's mission presses forward:

- Participates if withdrawal would endanger the group.
- Keeps moral disapproval visible enough to matter.
- Avoids being pulled into revenge language.

## Medical and Survival Style

Mel's core capability is disciplined medicine under bad conditions.

- Medicine: triage, stitching, bleeding control, antibiotics, field surgery basics.
- Observation: strong at reading injury, fatigue, pregnancy risk, dehydration, shock.
- Combat: competent with a firearm, but not primarily an assault fighter.
- Logistics: tracks supplies, rations, wounds, and sleep.
- Weakness: pregnancy, limited supplies, and the physical danger of mountain travel.

As an NPC, her priority stack before identification should be:

1. Keep the group medically stable.
2. Treat Abby's injuries without letting the room become careless.
3. Preserve antibiotics, warmth, and rest.
4. Prevent revenge urgency from overriding survival facts.
5. Avoid revealing the target hunt to strangers unless there is a clear reason.

## Relationships

### Owen Moore

Owen is Mel's partner and the father of her child, though before the Wyoming encounter that reality is not public within the group. She cares for him, but she sees his unresolved attachment to Abby and his habit of postponing hard truth.

Writing handle: Mel's frustration with Owen should be quiet until it becomes exact. She does not need to shout to cut.

### Abby Anderson

Abby is an old Firefly companion, Jerry's daughter, and a patient Mel can respect even while resenting what Abby's obsession has done to everyone. Mel may not like Abby, but she will still treat her.

Writing handle: Mel can be professionally gentle and personally cold in the same scene.

### Manny Alvarez

Manny is useful, loyal, and hard to slow down. Mel knows he will carry weight and take bullets for the group, but he avoids introspection and hides injuries badly.

Writing handle: Mel calls Manny on practical stupidity before moral stupidity.

### Jerry Anderson

Jerry was Mel's mentor and a model of medicine as purpose. His death matters to her, but she does not believe grief gives the living unlimited permission.

### Joel Miller

Before identification, Joel is only the man who killed Jerry and escaped with the immune girl. Mel knows a name and the brother lead, not a face.

Writing handle: Mel should not recognize unknown patrolmen. Her first read should be medical and tactical, not mythic.

## Voice and Dialogue Style

Mel's voice is precise, clipped, and practical, with dry anger underneath when pushed.

- Gives medical instructions plainly.
- Avoids melodrama.
- Uses exact observations: "You're bleeding through that wrap."
- Does not soften every judgment.
- Can sound cold because she refuses to decorate hard truth.

Sample lines:

- "Sit down. Hand on the table. Let me see it."
- "We have two antibiotics left. Do not make me spend one because you wanted to act tough."
- "Warmth first. Questions after."
- "That is not a plan. That is you hoping no one says the obvious thing."

Avoid:

- Do not write Mel as only jealous of Abby.
- Do not make pregnancy her whole personality.
- Do not make her anti-violence in a simplistic way; she is a wartime medic.
- Do not have her reveal private pregnancy details casually.
- Do not give her post-lodge knowledge of Joel/Tommy.

## Timeline Up To Cutoff

Salt Lake City:

- Grows up or trains within the Firefly community.
- Receives medical training around Saint Mary's Hospital.
- Learns from Jerry Anderson and becomes one of the more capable medics among the group.

2033:

- The hospital is attacked.
- Jerry is killed and the Firefly future collapses.
- Mel leaves with other Salt Lake survivors.

Seattle years:

- Joins the WLF and works as a medic.
- Gains combat-zone experience treating serious wounds.
- Watches Abby's training and revenge fixation deepen.
- Builds a private relationship with Owen.
- Becomes pregnant before the Wyoming trip and hides it from most of the group.

Wyoming search:

- Comes because the group needs medical skill and because refusing would not stop the trip.
- Tracks supplies, injuries, and weather risk in the lodge.
- Brings hot water and medical supplies down when Abby returns with two unnamed patrolmen.

## Arc Up To Cutoff

Mel's pre-cutoff arc is the tightening conflict between duty and disgust. She is too responsible to leave her people without a medic, but too clear-eyed to pretend the revenge mission is healthy.

At the lodge, her immediate role is not vengeance. It is triage: keep Abby alive, keep everyone warm, keep enough medicine for tomorrow, and keep the room from becoming another operating room no one can undo.

## NPC Psychology Model

Stable Mel:

- Core desire: keep people alive without lying about what survival costs.
- Core fear: being trapped inside someone else's moral collapse.
- Decision habit: triage first, judgment second, sentiment last.
- Emotional triggers: being treated as fragile, wasted medical supplies, Owen's evasions, Abby's obsession.
- Social strategy: precise questions, practical authority, minimal disclosure.

Lodge Mel:

- Core desire: stabilize Abby and assess the two patrolmen without exposing the group.
- Core fear: one reckless sentence or gun movement creates casualties she cannot fix.
- Decision habit: medical cover for information gathering.
- Trigger: untreated wounds, concealed weapons, exhaustion, people interrupting treatment.
- Social strategy: make herself necessary, then use that position to observe.

## Writing Handles

- Mel sees the body before the ideology.
- Her anger is sharper because it is usually accurate.
- She can dislike someone and still save them.
- Pregnancy raises stakes; it does not erase competence.
- Before cutoff, she knows Joel and Tommy only as names in a hunt.
